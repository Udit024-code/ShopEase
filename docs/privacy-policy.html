Privacy Policy for ShopEase

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShopEase ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the ShopEase mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Who operates ShopEase: ShopEase is operated by Udit Chaudhary. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at uditchaudhary0406@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate a shopping app:

CategoryDataWhy
AccountEmail address and a password (stored securely as a hash by our authentication provider — we never see your plaintext password)To create and secure your account and let you sign in
ProfileUsername, display name, bio, and an optional profile photo you uploadTo personalize your account
AddressesRecipient name, phone number, and postal address (line 1/2, city, state, postal code, country)To deliver orders you place
Shopping activityCart items, wishlist items, and order history (products, quantities, amounts, and payment method)To provide cart, wishlist, checkout, and order-history features
TechnicalBasic technical and diagnostic data required for the app to function and for security (e.g. authentication tokens on your device)To keep the App running and secure

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, biometric data, or advertising identifiers. The App currently supports Cash on Delivery only and does not collect or process card, bank, or other payment-instrument details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your account.
  • To show products, and to operate your cart, wishlist, checkout, and orders.
  • To deliver orders to the address you provide.
  • To secure the App and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. How Your Information Is Stored and Shared

Your data is stored using Supabase, our backend and hosting provider (database, authentication, and file storage). Data is protected with row-level security so that you can only access your own account data, and is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

We share information only with service providers that help us run the App:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, authentication, and storage of your account, order, and profile data
Email delivery providerSending transactional emails such as password-reset messages (only your email address is shared, and only to send you these messages)

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the App.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), we delete your account and associated personal data. Some records may be retained where required by law.

5. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the App: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and associated data — including your profile, saved addresses, cart, wishlist, and order history — and cannot be undone.

You may also request deletion by contacting us at uditchaudhary0406@gmail.com.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the App (edit your profile, manage addresses, delete your account), or contact us for assistance.

7. Children's Privacy

ShopEase is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access controls — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
